About This Project

QR-Based Resume & Certificate Verification System allows recruiters to verify student resumes and certificates using QR codes. Scan or browse the student list to access verified credentials.

Features

  • View complete list of all registered students
  • Preview and download student resumes
  • Access certificates with QR verification
  • Verify authenticity of certificates using AI
